# Aftershock — Product Requirements

The accepted product vision, condensed from `AFTERSHOCK_OPENCODE_HANDOFF.md` — the binding spec (§ references point to it). Entity/edge schema, API routes and status enums are defined in `docs/DATA_CONTRACTS.md` and are deliberately not duplicated here.

## Positioning (§1)
Evidence-integrity and research-impact workspace for professors, thesis writers, literature-review authors, students, research assistants and teams. Aftershock detects official changes to scientific publications, examines how the researcher's own documents cite them, and traverses claim-level evidence dependencies to identify passages and downstream conclusions requiring human review — presenting reasoning and sources in a real FalkorDB knowledge graph and helping the researcher decide how to respond. AI may propose analysis, find relevant publications and draft suggested revisions; humans control consequential scientific decisions.
Differentiation chain: **official source change → its specific citation use in my manuscript → the affected claim → dependent conclusions → human-reviewed outcome → clear report** — versus a generic retraction badge or citation-alert service. `cites` ≠ `discusses` ≠ `supports` ≠ `contrasts` ≠ method-reuse ≠ has-status-notice. The product does not certify scientific truth of any claim; a retracted paper may be cited historically without compromising the citing document.
Principles: accuracy over drama (transparent uncertainty, inspectable provenance); researcher control (sources read-only, AI drafts = suggestions); the graph is the reasoning substrate (real stored records and traversals, not a cosmetic constellation); simple by default, powerful on demand; historical honesty; no hidden limitations.

## Information architecture (§3)
Five primary sidebar areas, exactly: **Overview** (attention, recent meaningful source changes, continuation, import prompt, review coverage) · **Investigations** (focused citation/claim-impact path, focused graph, source inspection → review transitions) · **Library** (documents, bibliographies, DOI entries, publications, versions, optional collections, unresolved items) · **Review Queue** (human tasks, risk prioritization, Review Overview, Evidence Reader, revision workflow) · **Activity** (human-readable history, versions, source-change dates, audit events, reports, Impact Replay).
Secondary destination: **Knowledge Graph** with full global view; Investigations has its own focused graph. The Evidence Reader is a **shared workspace**, not a sixth area. Export is reachable from Review Overview, Investigations and Library, not from a sixth page.
**Universal Context Inspector**: selecting a document, publication, claim, citation use, graph edge or conclusion opens the same conceptual inspector — exact source/passage, notice state, citation role, impact reasoning, verified vs inferred origin, status, and one context-specific next action. No contradictory interpretations across pages.
Collections are optional (sensible auto name at first import; All Research + collection filters; publications may appear in multiple collections; not mandatory before first import; document-version and collection associations preserved).

## Import-first behavior (§4)
First-run landing = `import`; after import = `automatic`; review prioritization = `risk`.
Landing: **Choose a document** primary, drag-and-drop supported; **Bibliography** and **Single DOI** secondary; **Try guided example** visible without an account. Do not advertise that DOI/bibliography-only import produces passage-level dependencies for the user's own claims — claim-level impact requires original text/citation contexts. Truly supported formats: **PDF, DOCX, TXT** only, with honest format/size limits. Near upload: analysis starts automatically, the source remains unchanged, and real processing/third-party transmission/retention/limitations are disclosed from the implemented deployment — no fictional privacy assurances.
Stages (truthful status; no fabricated percentages, fake streaming AI logs or invented availability) — contracted as `AnalysisRun.stage` in DATA_CONTRACTS §4: 1 `file_selected` → 2 `uploading` (true progress or honest indeterminate; cancellable) → 3 `uploaded` (analysis auto-starts) → 4 `parsing` → 5 `resolving` identifiers + checking verified notices → 6 `classifying` citation uses and proposing evidence dependencies → 7 `writing_graph` (provenance-backed records) → 8 `results`.
Research Results show detected/matched/unmatched counts, verified notices, items needing human attention, and what remains unknown; primary CTA **Review affected evidence**; "No notice found" never implies scientific reliability or complete global coverage.
Ambiguous citation **Skip for now**: preserve original text/location/candidates/uncertainty; mark `Unresolved / Deferred`; no confirmed citation edge and no claim that a candidate's retraction affects the manuscript; the unresolved item appears in Library references, Review Queue source matching, and the graph as an unresolved reference linked only by appropriately marked candidate relationships. A possibly-retracted candidate may raise *matching priority* only, labeled **"possible source-status issue — verification required."** Later actions: compare candidates, confirm with provenance, choose none, or leave unresolved — re-evaluation rechecks only the relevant graph paths and preserves human choice history. Failures (unreadable PDF, partial extraction, missing/wrong/multiple DOI matches, unavailable sources, duplicates, interruption, free graph reset) preserve reliable partial information with targeted retry — never a bogus success or silently lost context.

## Review workflows (§7–9)
**Review Overview**: calm entry ("Two passages need your attention"), progress + **Start reviewing** + prominent secondary **Read annotated paper**; risk prioritization without blocking routine work. **Annotated paper** is the bird's-eye view: `Original PDF` overlay and `Accessible Reading` reflow share passages/annotations while original bytes stay unchanged; modes all/needs-attention/clean reading, minimap, prev/next, preserved scroll/selection on return. Highlight taxonomy: amber = needs review; muted blue/dotted = uncertain; muted red = verified source notice; **sage green `#658C79`** (pale surface `#E2EFE7`) = human reviewed — green implies neither verified truth nor an applied change. Hover is never the only explanation.
**Focused Evidence Reader**: read-only document · one claim · one explanation · one decision. Explain why it needs attention using the real notice, precise citation use and provenance. Three clear human decisions, save, show success, explicit **Next issue** (no auto-navigate). No authorized full text → metadata + notice + publisher links only; no invented quotes; highlights only where source spans were reliably recovered. Original documents are never rewritten — proposed revisions are separate, labeled drafts.
**Outcomes**: **Revision needed** (human review recorded; follow-up `revision_pending`; optional AI-assisted editable suggestion) · **More evidence needed** (investigation task; case stays open and uncertain) · **Not affected** (written explanation **required**; closes only the specific citation-use concern — never certifies the claim) · **Evidence updated** (sources, provenance and reviewer assessment required; attaching a candidate never closes a review).
**Orthogonal states** (kept separate; a case may be green *reviewed* while amber *revision pending*): `awaiting_review`, `review_recorded`, `further_investigation`, `revision_pending`, `draft_prepared`, `reported_applied_externally`, `resolved`, `reopened_due_to_new_information`. Copying a draft is not applying it; "I updated my manuscript elsewhere" is user-reported, never independently verified; new imports can be rechecked; reopening is non-destructive.
**Find Evidence** is claim-scoped (not a chatbot): confirm the question type, show candidates with short relevance rationale across supporting/contrasting/context-only/uncertain; no one-click "trusted" citation or bogus credibility percentages; the user attaches **assessments**, not proof. Evidence families (review articles, preprints, duplicate reports, shared datasets) group only on sourced or qualified relationships — five papers are never five independent confirmations without verified data origins. No relevant results → state the coverage and limits; never assert no evidence exists.

## Knowledge graph (§6 — semantics contracted in DATA_CONTRACTS §1–2)
The graph is **actual FalkorDB data** — displayed paths must be queryable records; reading seeded JSON alone is not fulfillment; no mocked/decorative graph, no edges for visual effect. Entity/edge types and their exact meaning (incl. confirmed `OCCURRENCE_MATCHES_PUBLICATION` vs explicitly unverified `POSSIBLE_MATCH`, verified `PUBLICATION_HAS_NOTICE`, per-role citation edges, `CONCLUSION_DEPENDS_ON_CLAIM`) are defined in DATA_CONTRACTS §1–§2. Non-negotiables: source notices ≠ reviewer decisions ≠ claim validity; AI-proposed relations are never displayed as confirmed facts; internal confidence is never a reliability certification; every material edge carries the provenance fields of DATA_CONTRACTS §2; legends and non-color styles distinguish direct support, historical mention, indirect dependency and uncertainty. UX: zoom/pan/filter/search/focus, local+global modes, one–two-hop expansion, evidence-path highlight, inspect-every-edge, readable list/table equivalent for keyboard/screen-reader users, reduced motion; replay uses recorded state or explicitly labeled reconstruction.

## Reports & exports (§10)
Three deliverables: **Evidence Impact Report** (default PDF; executive summary page 1, readable appendix), **Revision Pack** (DOCX preferred; original passage, location, proposed replacement, rationale, sources, explicit **NOT APPLIED** state), **Annotated Manuscript** (original-layout with annotations + accessible alternative; consistent, non-color-dependent legend). **Never block export because work is unfinished.** Every export prominently discloses overall review status, unresolved counts, last source-check timestamps, open follow-ups and extraction/search limitations; the existence of unresolved work may be hidden in appendices but never denied; "review workflow completed" is never "scientifically certified." Exports are versioned snapshots — not silently rewritten when research changes; preview identifies included sensitive content; a format that cannot be correctly produced is disabled rather than faked. Advanced optional Audit/Research Data Export carries edge-level provenance.

## Temporal honesty (§11)
Five timestamps remain distinct and user-visible: source notice's **actual publication date** · **first detected** by Aftershock · **last verified/checked** against source · **human decision date** · **export generated date** (data-model mapping: `docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`). Activity is a human-readable timeline; click-through inspects the original source/passage/review/graph path; routine metadata refresh does not pollute it. **Impact Replay** compares recorded knowledge/decision/graph snapshots — or an explicitly labeled reconstruction — never a fabricated past (no 2020 workspace snapshots claimed for a 2026 import). Later material notices can create follow-up reviews non-destructively while earlier decisions remain intact and inspectable.

## Design language & accessibility (§2)
Editorial, retro-academic aesthetic: warm paper/cream background, charcoal/dark-ink text, restrained purple/indigo for navigation/brand/focus, amber for review work, muted red for verified formal source notices, subtle muted blue for uncertainty, **sage green `#658C79`** for human-reviewed states (never implying a claim was verified true or a change applied). Expressive licensed serif for brand/display/headings, readable modern sans-serif for body/controls/graph labels — implemented with self-hosted OFL 1.1 fonts (Inter Variable, Source Serif 4 Variable; see README). High contrast, careful hierarchy, light-mode first; optional dark theme only if it displaces nothing critical. WCAG 2.2 AA aims: clear labels, 16–18px reading text, keyboard + screen-reader support, visible focus, generous targets, no hover-only functionality, motion reduction, accessible alternatives to graph and PDFs, truthful PDF coordinates with disclosed extraction limits. **Never create an age-based "simple mode"** — progressive disclosure, optional compact density, keyboard shortcuts. Responsive: one focused claim by default on laptop; compare view and inspector collapse as options; narrow screens get accessible tabbed/stacked reading.

## Scope split (§15)
**P0 essentials**: real source notice (verified Mehra/Lancet record), real FalkorDB traversal, clear Review Overview → annotated paper → focused reader, human decision with correct green/amber states, honest report export, deployed site — delivered as a polished, verifiable **guided example** (fixed fixture, §12) plus an honestly scoped real import path (M4). Sample-only support must never be labeled arbitrary-document support.
**P1 (M7, only after P0 passes)**: Find Evidence candidate assessment, grouped shared-data dependencies, richer revision pack, graph explorer controls, historical replay, full arbitrary-PDF reflow, expanded export. Full dual-mode PDF/reflow and sophisticated alternative-evidence discovery remain standing product requirements — P0 does not remove them from the product, it defers their general implementation.
